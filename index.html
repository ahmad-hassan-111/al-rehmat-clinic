About Al-Rehmat Clinic

Al-Rehmat Clinic & Physiotherapy Center is a physiotherapy clinic in Allama Iqbal Town, Bahawalpur. We help people recover from pain, injury and neurological conditions with personal, professional care.

Our Doctor

Dr. Asghar Mukhtar (PT) is the CEO and Lead Physiotherapist of the clinic. He holds a Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) and a Master of Science in Neurological Physical Therapy (MS-NPT), and he is an Assistant Professor at Sutlej College of Allied Health Sciences, Bahawalpur. His main focus is back pain, sciatica, pelvic pain and neurological rehabilitation.

Our Team

Female patients are treated privately and comfortably by the female physiotherapists on our staff.

Our Focus

Neurological rehabilitation is a core strength of the clinic, including recovery after stroke, Bell’s palsy, and developmental delays in children such as cerebral palsy.

Our Services

Physiotherapy and rehabilitation for pain, injury and neurological conditions.

  • Therapy to help people regain movement and independence after a stroke and in conditions such as Bell’s palsy.

  • Supportive therapy for children with developmental delays, including cerebral palsy.

  • Assessment and treatment of back pain and sciatica to reduce pain and improve movement.

  • Careful, private treatment for pelvic pain, with female physiotherapists available for female patients.

  • Guided recovery after surgery to rebuild strength, movement and confidence.

  • A physiotherapy technique used to treat muscle pain and tightness.

  • Customized exercises and techniques to improve how freely your joints move.

  • Structured exercise to build stamina and heart and lung fitness during recovery.

  • Not sure which service you need? The doctor will examine you first, explain the problem and recommend the right treatment.
